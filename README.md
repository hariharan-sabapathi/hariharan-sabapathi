## 🔍 What I work on

<details>
<summary><b>Analytics engineering</b> — models that stay right</summary>

<br>

Layered dbt models with schema and freshness tests. Star schemas in Snowflake with deliberate fact and dimension design. Semantic layers holding CLV, MRR, and churn logic in one place instead of scattered across ad-hoc queries — so two teams pulling the same metric get the same number. KPI definitions that survive contact with people who disagree about them.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Data engineering</b> — getting it there reliably</summary>

<br>

Kafka → PySpark → Delta Lake on S3 for streaming event data. Snowpark pipelines with automated quality gates. Flattening nested HL7 FHIR R4 NDJSON into queryable star schemas. BCNF schema design and SQL optimization — reusable reporting views and join tuning cut latency 65% on one warehouse build.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Analysis & experimentation</b> — is it real, or is it noise?</summary>

<br>

A/B testing with chi-square, 95% confidence intervals, and power analysis to size an effect before anyone ships on it. Cohort retention, RFM segmentation, regression forecasting, and two-sample t-tests for when a regional margin gap has to be shown real before it changes a quota.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>BI & reporting</b> — built around the decision, not the dataset</summary>

<br>

Power BI in depth: DAX scenario modeling, Power Query M, row-level security so each regional manager sees only their territory, scheduled gateway refresh. Tableau and Streamlit as well. The test is whether someone can read it without me standing next to them.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Healthcare analytics</b> — CMS, claims, and hospital operations</summary>

<br>

Denial control and A/R aging by payer and CARC category. ED throughput benchmarking across 4,660 hospitals from CMS Outpatient Quality Reporting data. HVBP reimbursement penalty modeling against Total Performance Scores. Patient flow, bed utilization, and readmission risk from FHIR clinical data.

</details>
